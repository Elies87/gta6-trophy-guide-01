Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, roughly 200 hours, difficulty about 4 out of 10. There are no online trophies, no difficulty settings and no mission replay. Free-roam continues after the story, so almost everything can be cleaned up late — the single exception is flagged in stage 1. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off a trophy run. Sources: PowerPyx roadmap, GTABase roadmap, Push Square roadmap.
